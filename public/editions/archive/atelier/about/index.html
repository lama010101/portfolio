About & Contact

A business person who builds with AI.

Thirty years of international business, now applied to conceiving, directing and evaluating AI-built products.

My story

I'm a French-American business and sales professional — a citizen of France and the United States — with an international perspective and an enduring interest in technology and innovation. I have lived and worked in the United States, Mexico, France and Thailand.

After a period as a family caregiver, I have spent the time since mid-2024 developing practical experience using AI to conceive, direct and build digital products independently — including BackToThePast (Guess-History), a multiplayer history game now live in alpha.

I am not a software engineer. I define the problem, make the product decisions, direct AI coding agents to build, and evaluate the result. I also produced "Let It Roll", an album made with Suno with AI-generated videos — the same builder's habit, applied to music.
