The game & the ambition

One product, two horizons — clearly separated.

Live · Alpha

What exists now

A multiplayer history and geography guessing game. I conceived it, defined the product and directed AI coding agents to build it — scoping each task, reviewing every change and setting safeguards against regressions.

  • Players guess real historical events and places.
  • A pipeline generates prompts from event themes and produces the images players guess from.
  • Several AI models can also play — their answers feed back into quality evaluation.
  • Multiplayer sessions, live in alpha today.

Under active development and verification — expect rough edges; that is what alpha means.

Ambition

Where it could go

The ambition — not a current capability — is a structured, curated historical knowledge platform supporting education and AI model evaluation.

  • Historical knowledge benchmarking.
  • Historical event verification.
  • AI factual accuracy evaluation.
  • Educational products.
  • Historical content licensing.

The longer-term goal: a community-built database of verified real historical events.
