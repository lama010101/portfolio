How I work with AI

AI is not just a tool I use. It is how I build.

My contribution is not asking AI questions. It is defining the problem, directing the build and refusing to accept work that is wrong.

The method

Business problem→ Product definition→ AI orchestration→ Implementation→ Validation→ Iteration

  1. Identify a problem or opportunity.
  2. Define the business objective.
  3. Explore possible solutions with AI.
  4. Translate the solution into precise requirements.
  5. Direct AI coding agents.
  6. Evaluate the implementation.
  7. Challenge errors and inconsistencies.
  8. Iterate until objectives are met.

At every step the split is the same: I decide; AI executes. The interactive version of this loop is on the home page.
