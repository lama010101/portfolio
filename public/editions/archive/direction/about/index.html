My story

Commercial experience.
An enduring interest in what’s next.

I’m a French-American business and sales professional with an international perspective and an enduring interest in technology and innovation.

Over the past 18 months, I’ve developed practical experience using AI to conceive, direct and build digital products independently, including BackToThePast (Guess-History).

My approach combines commercial reasoning with the ability to work closely with AI systems and coding agents. I enjoy exploring possibilities, challenging assumptions and turning ideas into working solutions.

I am not a software engineer. I define the problem, make the product decisions, direct AI to build and evaluate the result.
