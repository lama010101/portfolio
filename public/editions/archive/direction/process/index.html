04 / HOW I WORK WITH AI

AI is not just a tool I use. It is how I build.

Direction over automation

The method / Interactive sequence

From business intent to a working result.

Select a stage to inspect the role it plays. The loop is deliberately iterative: evaluation feeds better direction.

Business problem

Start with the opportunity, not the technology.

Identify a real problem or opportunity. Clarify who it matters to and what a useful outcome looks like.
