The build loop / 08 moves

AI is not just a tool I use. It is how I build.

My contribution is more than asking AI questions. It is deciding what should exist, directing the work and refusing to accept output that misses the objective.

  1. 01

    Identify the problem or opportunity.

  2. 02

    Define the business objective.

  3. 03

    Explore solutions with AI.

  4. 04

    Translate into precise requirements.

  5. 05

    Direct AI coding agents.

  6. 06

    Evaluate the implementation.

  7. 07

    Challenge errors and inconsistencies.

  8. 08

    Iterate until objectives are met.
