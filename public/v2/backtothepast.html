A — The game

Guess the event.
Beat the humans — and the models.

The product I direct AI to build: a live multiplayer game where historical knowledge is the scoreboard — for players and for AI.

BackToThePast (also known as Guess-History) is a multiplayer guessing game built around real historical events. The pipeline I designed generates prompts from event themes, produces the associated images, lets players guess the events — and then lets several AI models play the same rounds and evaluate the outputs for quality.

In other words: the game entertains humans while quietly producing exactly the kind of data my evaluation work is about — how well AI systems handle history, language and factual accuracy.

How a round works
  • An event theme is selected and turned into AI-generated prompts and images
  • Players guess the historical event — and its place on the map
  • Multiple players compete in the same round
  • AI models play alongside humans and score the outputs
  • Results feed a community goal: a database of verified real events
