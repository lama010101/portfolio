01 — Story

From selling machines
to shipping software.

I started in international B2B sales — industrial equipment, packaging machinery, technology — running projects from first tender to final acceptance across Latin America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. I founded and ran an import business in Mexico for a decade, then co-founded a data-visualization startup in the US and France.

Along the way I kept ending up on the quality side: writing functional specifications and test plans, making UI/UX calls, coordinating offshore development teams — and eventually spending two stints as a QA Test Engineer on internationalization teams at Google and Apple, testing products for the world's languages.

Since mid-2024 that thread pulled tight. I began building digital products the only way that made sense for me: I conceive the product, write the requirements, direct AI coding agents to implement, and stay personally accountable for what ships. BackToThePast is live in alpha. This website is the latest thing I've directed into existence.

What I'm known for
  • Multilingual product quality — EN/FR/ES at native level
  • Functional specs, test plans & acceptance criteria
  • International B2B sales & commercial negotiation
  • Startup founding, product ownership & team coordination
  • Directing AI agents — prompts, guardrails, review, iteration
